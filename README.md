# Archery King · Bullseye Challenge

Free single-file HTML5 archery game. Hold to draw the bow, drag to fine-aim,
release to loose — mind the wind flag, it drifts your arrow sideways.
10 arrows per round, rings score 10/8/6/4/2, graded F to S. One HTML file,
zero assets.

**Play:** https://shuangyueliao.github.io/archery-king/

- Two-stage control: oscillating power bar (timing) + drag aiming (precision),
  mouse / touch / keyboard (Space to draw, arrows to aim)
- Wind drift with a waving direction flag and strength chevrons; arrows 6-10
  bring a vertically moving target and stronger wind
- Arrow flight with camera zoom, arrows stick in the target with impact wobble
- Per-arrow score detail, total grade (F/D/C/B/A/S), best score + grade saved
  locally, sound toggle
- Procedural graphics & WebAudio sounds, no dependencies, no tracking
